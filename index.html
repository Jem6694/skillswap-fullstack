Why SkillSwap exists

Students should not have to navigate college alone.

Many students want to learn skills outside their courses, meet people with similar goals, and become more involved on campus—but they may not know where to begin.

SkillSwap was created to make peer learning, faculty discovery, networking, club involvement, and campus resources easier to find through one personalized platform.

Our mission
Make campus knowledge and opportunity easier for every student to discover.

SkillSwap is currently a student-built prototype and is not presented as an official university system.

Frequently asked questions

New to SkillSwap?

These answers explain how the current prototype works and what students can expect.

What does the AI match percentage mean?

It estimates how closely one student’s teaching skills align with another student’s learning goals. It is a recommendation, not a guarantee of a successful connection.

Is SkillSwap a tutoring service?

SkillSwap supports peer learning and networking. It does not replace official tutoring, advising, or academic support services.

How will professors be included?

Faculty profiles would be opt-in or university-approved and could display public expertise, courses, research interests, office hours, and preferred contact methods.

How will club recommendations work?

Recommendations will compare a student’s major, interests, goals, and desired skills with the focus and activities of participating organizations.

Does SkillSwap access grades or private records?

The current prototype does not access grades, course records, or private university accounts. Any future institutional integration would require university approval and appropriate privacy protections.

Can students add any skill?

Yes. The current system accepts comma-separated skills. A future version will offer searchable skill categories while continuing to support custom entries.
